What lives in the backyard

Features built for playing together.

The SMP stays recognizably Minecraft while adding small systems that make a private community world nicer to live in.

Automatic

Open Doors

Players can join without owning a premium Minecraft account.

Movement

Cottage Boost

Elytra flight and spear lunging give movement a little extra personality.

Tutorial: Use an Elytra while using a spear with the Lunge enchantment.

Automatic

Grand Arrivals

Players get special animations when entering the world.

Villagers

Friendly Neighbors

Convenient villager interactions for moving them around your builds.

Tutorial: Crouch and right-click a villager.

Customization

Cottage Custom

Pose and customize armor stands for decorative builds.

Tutorial: Use a tripwire hook to configure item displays.

Protection

Cottage Protection

Protect builds and belongings with player claims.

Tutorial: Hold a golden shovel and use /claimhelp.

Utility

Cozy Repairs

A more convenient Mending experience.

Tutorial: Crouch and right-click a tool with Mending.

Teams

Cottage Teams

Team up with other players and share a little corner of the world.

Tutorial: /team create <name>.

Comfort

Cozy Sitting

Sit around the world using /sit, or interact with supported blocks and players.

Travel

Wanderer's Way

Type /rtp to teleport to a random location and find somewhere new.

Survival

True Hardcore

Keep Inventory is disabled, so death still matters.

Building

Cottage Displays

Decorative display tools for builders.

Tutorial: Java clients need the Dream Displays mod.

Harvesting

Cottage Harvest

Harvest eligible connected wood and ores while sneaking. Stripped logs are excluded.

Voice

Cottage Voice

Proximity voice chat for hanging out while playing.

Tutorial: Bedrock players can use /dvc start.

Special

The Cottage Mace

A one-of-a-kind server setup built specifically for this world.

Death

Memorial Keepsakes

A player's head is left behind when they die as a little memorial of their time in the world.

Keep the backyard nice.

The community rules apply here too. Minecraft-specific rules are handled separately through mcrules?.

The Cottage★ is built around people first. Treat the people in the backyard with the same respect you would give them in the main home.
Respect everyone

No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or targeted behavior.

Keep it appropriate

No sexual, excessively disturbing, or otherwise inappropriate content.

No disruption

Don't intentionally disrupt the community, spam, abuse mentions, or misuse server features.

Protect privacy

Don't share private information, screenshots, conversations, or personal details without permission.

No malicious content

No malware, phishing, scams, malicious links, or attempts to compromise another person's account.

Play fair

Follow the Minecraft rules listed under mcrules?.

Enforcement: Depending on the situation, breaking the rules may result in a warning, timeout, kick, or ban.
