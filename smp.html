SMP Features

Click Villagers

Right click and/or crouch to pick up villagers, reset their trades, change their biomes, claim them and much more!

GriefPrevention

Run "/claimhelp" on chat for more info. (That's literally it, the tutorial is in the GUI ingame.)

Better Mending

Crouch + Right click to mend your tools/armors that have mending enchantment on them. (Uses your ingame XP so you NEED to have XP.)

Ultimate Teams

Genuinely js "/team gui" or if you're a miserable fuck like @Ayudalo just use the commands.

Player Heads Drop

Players can drop their heads when they die, allowing you to collect them as trophies.

TPA & RTP

Send a Teleport Request to players, & if you're antisocial, /rtp to randomly teleport to any location at any dimension.

Veinminer

Veinmines Logs (no stripped logs) & Ores (You can also request to add a block to Veinminer with @Makkrabb)

GSit

"/sit" or Crouch + Right clicking.

Feature Tutorials

GriefPrevention

Use /claimhelp in chat to open the in-game claiming tutorial.

Better Mending

Crouch + right click while holding your mending equipment to repair it using your XP.

Ultimate Teams

Use /team gui to open the team menu, or use the available team commands.

TPA & RTP

Use the teleport commands to request a teleport to another player or randomly teleport around the world.

Veinminer

Use the Veinminer key or command to mine connected ores and logs at once.

Click Villagers

Crouch and/or right click villagers to access their additional features.

GSit

Use /sit, or crouch + right click to sit.
