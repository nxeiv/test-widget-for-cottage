The Cottage★ SMP Features

Current public feature names. Click-free, easy-to-scan descriptions with the player-facing tutorial where one is available.

Automatic

Open Doors

Players can join without owning a premium Minecraft account.

Movement

Cottage Boost

Provides Elytra flight and Spear Lunging.

Tutorial: Use an Elytra while using a spear with the Lunge enchantment.

Automatic

Grand Arrivals

Players get special animations when entering the world.

Villagers

Friendly Neighbors

Provides convenient interactions with villagers.

Tutorial: Crouch and right-click a villager to place them into your inventory.

Customization

Cottage Custom

Lets players customize and pose armor stands.

Tutorial: Use a tripwire hook to configure item displays.

Special

The Cottage Mace

The server's special one-of-a-kind Mace setup.

Death

Memorial Keepsakes

A player's head is left behind when they die as a memorial keepsake.

Survival

Classic Crafting

Netherite is unavailable on the server.

Utility

Cozy Repairs

Provides a more convenient Mending experience.

Tutorial: Crouch and right-click a tool with Mending.

Protection

Cottage Protection

Lets players protect their builds and belongings.

Tutorial: Hold a golden shovel and use /claimhelp for claim details.

Teams

Cottage Teams

Lets players team up with other players.

Tutorial: Create a team with /team create <name>. Use /team for more details.

Comfort

Cozy Sitting

Lets players sit around the world.

Tutorial: Use /sit or right-click stairs, slabs, and players. Use /gsit toggle off to disable accidental sitting.

Teleport

Cottage Teleport

Lets players send teleport requests.

Tutorial: Use /tpa <player>.

Travel

Wanderer's Way

Type /rtp in chat to teleport in a random location.

Automatic

Lantern Light

Held light sources can illuminate the surroundings.

Quality of Life

Fresh Vanilla

Familiar vanilla gameplay with carefully chosen quality-of-life improvements.

Hardcore

True Hardcore

Keep Inventory is disabled, so death matters on the server.

Automatic

Steady Stats

Player attributes work correctly on the server.

Building

Cottage Displays

Provides decorative displays for builds.

Tutorial: Requires the Dream Displays mod on the Java client.

Harvesting

Cottage Harvest

Harvest eligible connected wood and ores while sneaking. Stripped logs are excluded.

Voice

Cottage Voice

Provides proximity voice chat.

Tutorial: Java players need the required voice-chat support installed. Bedrock players can use /dvc start.

◈ The Cottage★ Code of Conduct

Community standards for The Cottage★ Discord and SMP.

⌁ The Minecraft-specific rules are listed separately under mcrules?. Please follow both sets of rules while participating in The Cottage★.

Ⅰ. ◇ Respect Everyone

Treat members with respect. No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or targeted behavior.

Ⅱ. ◇ Keep It Appropriate

No sexual, excessively disturbing, or otherwise inappropriate content.

Ⅲ. ◇ No Spam or Disruption

Don't flood chats, abuse mentions, intentionally disrupt conversations, or misuse bots and server features.

Ⅳ. ◇ Keep Personal Information Private

Don't share private information, screenshots, conversations, or personal details without permission.

Ⅴ. ◇ No Malicious Content

No malware, phishing, scams, malicious links, or attempts to compromise another person's account.

Ⅵ. ◇ Respect Staff

Follow reasonable moderator instructions. If you disagree with a decision, discuss it privately rather than starting a public argument.

Ⅶ. ◇ Play Fair

Follow the Minecraft rules in mcrules? while playing on The Cottage★ SMP.

Ⅷ. ◇ Use Common Sense

If something is clearly intended to harm, disrupt, deceive, or exploit the community, don't do it—even if it isn't specifically listed above.

⌁ Enforcement
Breaking the rules may result in a warning, timeout, kick, or ban depending on the situation.

Respect the community. Protect the experience. Have fun.
