📖 The Cottage★ SMP Rules

The Cottage★ SMP is a Whitelist-Only server following the Invite-Only policy of The Cottage★ Discord server.

⚠️ Note: If you are banned from the Minecraft server, you will most likely also be banned from the Discord server.

Please read the Discord rules before playing.

1. 🚫 No Griefing

Intentionally destroying, modifying, or damaging another player's builds is prohibited.

Punishment: Level reset and/or temporary ban depending on severity.

2. 💰 No Stealing

Do not take items that belong to another player. Return stolen items when asked.

Punishment: 3 warnings. Refusing to return stolen items may result in an inventory clear.

3. ❌ No X-Ray

X-Ray and similar cheats are strictly prohibited.

Punishment: Temporary ban, starting at a minimum of 12 hours and increasing depending on severity.

4. ⚠️ No Duping

Duplication glitches, exploits, or other methods of duplicating items are strictly prohibited.

Punishment: Ban.

5. 🚷 No Inappropriate Builds

Offensive, explicit, or otherwise inappropriate builds are prohibited.

Punishment: Temporary ban depending on severity.

6. 🛑 No Lag Machines

Intentionally creating lag or excessive server load is prohibited.

Punishment: Ban depending on severity.

7. 🔗 No Malicious or Unauthorized External Links

Malware, phishing, malicious downloads, and other harmful links are prohibited. Ask a moderator before sharing external links.

Punishment: Ban.

8. 🖥️ Cheat Clients Are Prohibited

Cheat clients and other unfair modifications are not allowed. Players suspected of cheating may be investigated by moderators. Refusing to switch to an allowed client when instructed may result in a ban.

9. 🚫 No Racial Slurs or Hate Speech

Racial slurs, hate speech, and targeted hateful behavior are prohibited. Treat everyone with respect.

Punishment: Ban.

10. 🐉 Do Not Kill the Ender Dragon

The Ender Dragon is reserved for official server events. Please wait for the community Ender Dragon fight.

11. ⚔️ No Gangs

Clans are welcome. Toxic, abusive, or hostile gangs are not.

12. 👋 Welcome New Players

Notify the appropriate staff member when a new player joins. Help new players get started and make them feel welcome.

13. 🗣️ Respect Server Meetings

Do not fight during official server meetings. PvP may be disabled during meetings.

14. ⚔️ No Spawn Killing

Repeatedly killing players at spawn is prohibited. Ask for a duel before engaging in PvP. Unauthorized PvP may result in level penalties.

15. 🏳️ Official Clans

Official clans require at least 3 members. Clan wars must be supervised by moderators.

16. 🚪 No Combat Logging

Leaving the server during an active duel or combat situation is prohibited.

Punishment: Timeout or temporary ban depending on severity.

17. 🧑‍🌾 Don't Kill Trading Hall Villagers

Villagers in public trading halls are protected.

Punishment: Ban depending on severity.

18. 💬 Keep Chat Appropriate

Public Minecraft chat is logged in the Discord server. Use /w or /msg for private conversations. Keep conversations respectful and appropriate.

19. 🐾 Don't Kill Pets

Named pets are protected. If you accidentally kill someone's pet, inform the owner immediately.

20. 👑 No Abuse of Power

Creative permissions and builder roles are given based on trust. Abusing commands, permissions, or other staff privileges is prohibited.

Punishment: Ban depending on severity.

21. 🌱 No Seed Maps or Seed Cracking

Using seed maps, cracked seeds, or tools intended to reveal the world seed is prohibited. Play fairly and explore the world naturally.

🏡 Have Fun!

These rules exist to keep The Cottage★ SMP enjoyable, fair, and welcoming for everyone.

If you're unsure whether something is allowed, ask a moderator before doing it.
