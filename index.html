The home

What is The Cottage★?

Not a public server. Not a numbers game. A private corner of the internet built around the people who are here.

It starts with the people.

The Cottage★ is primarily a friend group — an invite-only Discord community made up of people from different backgrounds, places, cultures, and parts of life.

There isn't one thing everyone comes here for. We talk, call, share random things, play games, disappear for a while, come back, and generally spend time together. Some days are loud. Some days are quiet. Both still count.

Minecraft is one of the things we do together, but it isn't the definition of the community. Someone can be a huge part of The Cottage★ without ever opening Minecraft.
