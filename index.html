The home

What is The Cottage★?

Not a public server. Not a numbers game. A private corner of the internet built around the people who are here.

It starts with the people.

The Cottage★ is primarily a friend group — an invite-only Discord community made up of people from different backgrounds, places, cultures, and parts of life.

There isn't one thing everyone comes here for. We talk, call, share random things, play games, disappear for a while, come back, and generally spend time together. Some days are loud. Some days are quiet. Both still count.

Minecraft is one of the things we do together, but it isn't the definition of the community. Someone can be a huge part of The Cottage★ without ever opening Minecraft.

Where it came from

How the Cottage became home.

The Cottage★ grew from an older community, a friendship that survived a few difficult chapters, and the decision to build something new together.

08/01/24

The original community was created.

On 08/01/24, Mjay created the server that would eventually become part of The Cottage★'s history. Before it was known as The Cabin, it had an older name that is no longer remembered.

The idea came after Mjay left another server following a fight with its owner. He decided to create a new community of his own, becoming the owner while Gree became co-owner.

REVIVAL

The old community came back.

Later, Gree brought up the idea of reviving the old server. The community was brought back and renamed The Cabin, giving the group a fresh start while keeping the friendships that had formed around it.

THE PEOPLE

Uxer and Cosmic were found through Rate My Avatar.

After the server was revived, the group wanted to recruit new people. Gree found the Roblox game Rate My Avatar and used it to meet potential members.

Uxer and Cosmic were among the first and most important members recruited from that same game, becoming part of the early group that kept the community active.

THE BREAK

Then the owners fell out.

Months later, Mjay, Gree, and Kelly had a serious falling-out. Mjay removed Gree and Kelly from the Cabin, along with other people who were close to them.

Gree and Kelly decided to build something of their own — a new server they hoped could become something even better than the Cabin.

07/26/25

The Cottage★ was created.

On 07/26/25, The Cottage★ was created. Kelly became the owner, while Gree and Gree's sister became co-owners.

For a little while, the split was still very real. There was even a joking early rule that Mjay was not allowed in the Cottage. It was very much a “we are still mad” era.

AFTER

The fight ended. The family stayed.

A few days later, Gree, Kelly, and Mjay forgave each other. The cousins became family again, and the reason for the new server no longer needed to be a feud.

Mjay eventually decided to retire from being the owner, and the group chose to move forward together in the Cottage.

NOW

And the story keeps going.

The Cottage★ is now more than the server that came after the Cabin. It is the home built by the people who stayed, returned, forgave each other, met new friends, and kept making memories together.
